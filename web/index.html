How are these calculated?

Range = High − Low

Body = |Close − Open|

Upper wick = High − max(Open, Close)

Lower wick = min(Open, Close) − Low

Each percentage = part ÷ range × 100.

A zero-range candle has undefined percentages. Displayed percentages are rounded to two decimal places.
